Durga Mind App

Free Mental Health App · No App Store Needed
Works Offline · Private & Confidential

🤖 Install on Android

1
Open Chrome browser on your Android phone
2
Visit ddurga.dpdns.org
3
Tap the "Install App" banner that appears at the bottom
4
Tap "Install" — done! ✅
🚀 Open Site to Install

🍎 Install on iPhone

1
Open Safari browser on your iPhone
2
Visit ddurga.dpdns.org
3
Tap the Share button (box with arrow ↑) at the bottom
4
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5
Tap "Add" — done! ✅
🚀 Open Site to Install
